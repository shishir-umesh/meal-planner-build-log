# 2026-09-28: Kickoff

## Done

- **Market scan.** Looked at 15 meal-planning, pantry and grocery apps. Every feature I want exists somewhere, but no app connects pantry, plan, prep schedule, what we actually ate and restocking into one loop. No app schedules prep or treats takeout as a planned slot.
- **PRD.** 81 requirements across 11 modules, each marked MVP, beta or launch, plus personas, journeys, a data model and a gated roadmap.
- **Architecture.** Chose a local-first design with zero running cost: everything on the phone, free-tier sync between household phones.
- **One surprise.** No grocery service offers a public API for checking out on a user's behalf. The best available is handing a prefilled list to Instacart, or adding items to a Kroger cart. "The app does the shopping" becomes "the app fills the cart."
